# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Colombia who want to record and organize personal finances across accounts, cash, and optional pockets. The product uses COP and America/Bogota as its initial operating context.

## Product Purpose

Provide a private, understandable place to record, organize, find, and correct personal financial movements. The primary job is to make everyday movements easy to record and their current context easy to understand.

## Positioning

The working direction is “money in context”: connect what happened, where the money is held, and which category allocation it affects. The owner likes this direction; the public proposition still needs validation against verified financial behavior and intended users.

The owner selected **PlaENC** (from “Plata en Contexto”) as the working name. Its pronunciation, legal availability, final logo, and public rollout are still unapproved. A coherent wider identity should be developed around the selected name and approved mark. Existing colors and typography are candidates for adaptation, not fixed commitments.

## Operating Context

- Spanish-language personal finance experience for an initial Colombian audience.
- Users manually record movements and may later review assisted OCR/email entries before confirmation.
- Accounts and optional pockets hold money; assigned categories organize location-level allocations.
- Privacy is a cross-phase product requirement. Do not imply universal bank connectivity or guaranteed financial outcomes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary benefit: record and organize personal financial movements.
- Existing capabilities are part of the product and should be improved rather than described as beta or removed by default.
- Gmail/OCR should be enabled progressively only after reliability, privacy, and resource checks.
- Free resources only are authorized for now.
- A read-only conversational finance assistant is a future opportunity, not a current public promise or approved implementation. Do not make AI the central brand promise.
- Product UI remains in Spanish. Product copy should be financially precise, calm, empathetic, and nonjudgmental.
- Financial calculations, privacy, RLS, and confirmed-transaction rules must remain accurate; verify before making public claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Use PlaENC as the owner-selected working name; complete pronunciation and name-clearance checks before public rollout.
- Create the final logo from scratch after review of the exploratory logo concepts.
- Build a coherent identity around the selected name/logo; review existing colors and typography as optional source material.
- Preferred strategic territory: money in context, subject to validation.
- Keep personal financial information private and user-controlled.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing source code contains a generic wallet glyph and inconsistent product labels/theme colors across metadata and PWA files.
- Current design uses forest green, ivory, clay accents, serif display headings, and an Inter/system body stack. This visual language is not approved as the future brand.
- Public market descriptions already use categories, budgets, pockets, tracking, voice/OCR, and AI assistants. These features alone are not proven differentiators.
- PlaENC is the working name selected by the owner; no final logo, trademark clearance, domain availability, or brand user research is established.

## Product Principles

1. Explain money in its real account, pocket, category, and time context.
2. Keep the person in control of records and automated suggestions.
3. Make financial language precise without blame or shame.
4. Treat privacy as a product behavior and release gate.
5. Make public promises only when the implemented behavior has evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Spanish, readable financial figures, and accessible responsive web interactions. Avoid shaming language, stereotypes, and unexplained financial jargon.
